Why timing matters

Waiting costs nothing today. It costs later.

  • ·Premiums are generally priced by age, so locking in earlier usually keeps the rate lower for longer.
  • ·Insurability can change with your health, so being eligible today does not guarantee being eligible later.
  • ·The real cost of waiting is not a countdown timer. It is the gap your family is exposed to in the meantime.
